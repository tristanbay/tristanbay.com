date: 2023-04-01

These are various scripts/code scraps I wrote in C that relate to microtonality, and they're public domain. They include a note name generator for different equal temperaments, a program to convert just musical intervals into their prime factors, and more. You can find them [here](https://github.com/tristanbay/microtuning-scripts).
